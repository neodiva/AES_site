How to read the numbers

Do not compare raw throughput across a standalone S-box and a complete AES datapath as if they were identical workloads. Likewise, ASIC gate/area results and FPGA LUT counts are not directly interchangeable.

The useful comparison is multidimensional: technology + architecture + workload + throughput + area + power/energy + latency + security evaluation.

05

Physical & side-channel attacks

Observe leakage

Power: CPA/DPA correlate measured traces with hypothetical internal values.

EM: electromagnetic emanations can reveal switching-related information.

Timing: data-dependent timing can leak information; RISC-V crypto specifications include data-independent timing requirements for relevant instructions.

Disturb / access hardware

Fault injection can use clock/voltage glitches, EM or laser techniques. Invasive techniques may involve direct physical access.

Security wording: say exactly what was evaluated. “CPA-resistant under the evaluated setup” is more defensible than “resistant to physical attacks.”
